# INNtelligence — Founding Member Program

## What we are building

INNtelligence (by The Gracious Collection, LLC) is boutique hospitality intelligence —
an AI-driven Rate Intelligence Center built specifically for the 8-to-40-room
independent inn. Most revenue management platforms target hotel chains with
1,000+ rooms and price tags to match. We're solving the same problem at
the scale and price that boutique innkeepers actually run.

The platform is live and operating today against the Anchorage 1770 Inn
in Beaufort, SC. Across 4 room types and 90 forward days, it generates
360 daily rate recommendations, pushes approved rates to 7 OTAs via
SiteMinder in 2.3 seconds, runs autopilot publishing within owner-set
limits, drafts demand-triggered email campaigns to lapsed VIPs, and
surfaces 16 active alerts on competitor moves, occupancy gaps, and
festival windows.

Everything described here is built and verified — not roadmap.

## Why founding members

We need 3–5 inns running the platform alongside Anchorage 1770 before

## What the platform does, concretely

For each property, every day, the system:

1. Pulls last 24 months of occupancy + booking pace from your PMS
2. Forecasts demand for the next 90 days using 4 signals (booking pace 35%, seasonal pattern 25%, local events 20%, competitive set 20%)
3. Generates a recommended rate per room × per date, with confidence score
4. Compares against the live competitor set (auto-discovered nearby boutique hotels)
5. Drafts demand-triggered email campaigns for soft windows
6. Publishes approved rates to Booking.com, Expedia, Airbnb, VRBO, Hotels.com, Trip.com, and Agoda via SiteMinder (or directly to Booking.com / Expedia if you don't use a channel manager)
7. Fires alerts on competitor rate drops, surge windows, festivals, gap nights
8. Optionally auto-publishes within your set limits (max ±15% change, ≥75 confidence, 6 AM–10 PM, ≤3 changes/day)
